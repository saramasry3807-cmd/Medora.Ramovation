## 💡 Project Description
Medora Marketing Campaign: A 2-month B2B marketing & lead generation strategy to launch Medora (Ramovation's clinic management SaaS) in Egypt. Focuses on brand awareness, content strategy, Meta/Google ads, SEO, and direct sales outreach.

## 📅 Work Plan & Sprints
1. **Sprint 01:** Market & Marketing Research, Competitor Benchmarking (Dentolize, Sonamak, Smart Clinic).
2. **Sprint 02:** Business Model Canvas (BMC) & Value Proposition Architecture.
3. **Sprint 03:** Content Calendar, Copywriting & Visual Asset Design.
4. **Sprint 04:** Media Buying Campaign Setup (Meta Ads — EGP 1,000/week) & Lead-to-Patient Funnel.
5. **Sprint 05:** Partner & Creator Commission Playbooks (10%–20% Tiers).
6. **Sprint 06:** Final Presentation & Deliverable Submission.

---

## 🎯 Roles & Responsibilities
* **Market & Competitor Intelligence:** Deep-dive audit into clinic & polyclinic SaaS in Egypt & GCC.
* **Funnel & Offer Structuring:** Structuring direct vs. agency bundle pricing and starter incentives.
* **Campaign & Creative Direction:** Aligning tone of voice with Medora Brand Guidelines v1.0.
* **Documentation & Version Control:** Maintaining GitHub repository compliance, version logs, and project deliverables.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPL):** Maintaining efficient CPL on Meta Ads within the EGP 1,000/week budget.
* **Lead-to-Demo Conversion Rate:** Targeting a 15–20% conversion from WhatsApp inquiries to 15-minute live demos.
* **Demo-to-Trial / Close Rate:** Closing Starter (EGP 630–800/mo) and Standard (EGP 7,200–9,000/yr) subscriptions.
* **Agency Partner Acquisition:** Onboarding target marketing agencies under the 0% bundle / 10–20% commission tiers.
